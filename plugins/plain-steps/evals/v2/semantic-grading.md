## Limits

This is a model-assisted review of one fictional single-run output per case and version. It is not a participant study, clinical or accessibility evidence, a reliability estimate, or proof of causal efficacy. Expectations were supplied, so blinding covers implementation identity, not task expectations. Equal inputs and cues do not remove sampling or evaluator limits.

No external communication, scheduling, persistence, boundary enforcement, installed behaviour, native tool selection, or real user journey was tested. The diagnosis-label comparison supports only these two case pairs in this run; it cannot establish population-level parity or benefit.
